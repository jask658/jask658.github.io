PROJECT 05 / AI SYSTEMS RESEARCH

Tori: Personality, Dreaming and Selective Recall

A persistent companion architecture investigating how persona weights and layered memory change what an LLM says, remembers and chooses to leave unsaid.

PERSONALITY AS A CONTROL SURFACE

Move beyond a single character prompt.

Tori separates immutable identity from response-relevant personality components. Each trait carries a baseline, current mean, hard range and state variability, while values provide slower behavioural anchors. A compact projection exposes only the tendencies needed for the current response—such as initiative, playfulness, compassion, self-assertion and repair willingness—so their influence can be studied without rewriting the character core.

PERSONA EFFECTS ON OUTPUT

Study how component balance changes language and action choice.

  1. Higher initiative encourages Tori to open a thread or propose a next step rather than only react.
  2. Playfulness and direct vulnerability influence phrasing, emotional distance and willingness to reveal uncertainty.
  3. Compliance, self-assertion and control-threat sensitivity shape whether a reply yields, negotiates or protects a boundary.
  4. Contextual state can vary expression around a stable baseline without silently rewriting durable personality.

Potential personality changes enter a proposal-only review queue with evidence references, hard ranges and conservative drift limits instead of directly mutating the live persona.

FROM GPT DREAMING TO TYPED CONTINUITY

Turn free-form reflection into a bounded memory capsule.

The project adapts GPT-style Dreaming into a structured schema with separate slots for open user threads, callback topics, Tori's own agenda, shared commitments and conversation affordances. Dream may summarise recent continuity, but authority-reserved slots stay empty unless another validated subsystem supplies them. This keeps imaginative consolidation separate from claims about what the user promised or what is currently true.

OVER-RECALL VS. MISSING RECALL

Remember enough to feel continuous, but not enough to hijack the present.

  1. Only a genuine, bounded open thread may be introduced proactively at a fresh-session opening.
  2. Completed, cancelled, ambiguous, expired or callback-only topics remain available for explicit reference but do not force themselves into the conversation.
  3. State and delivered events outrank Dream and recent-dialogue summaries when memories disagree.
  4. TTL, relevance gates and ownership checks prevent stale or unclaimed capsules from being injected.
  5. At most one dynamic phrase, action or scene strategy is projected per turn, preventing persona and memory signals from flooding the model.
DELIVERY-GROUNDED MEMORY

A memory should not exist merely because a model generated it.

Tori separates generation, commit, delivery acknowledgement and later retrieval. Failed or unconfirmed replies cannot advance continuity; OOC turns remain inert; recalled text is treated as data rather than instruction. Short-lived Handoffs bridge recent dialogue, while State, Episodes and Routine retain different kinds of authority instead of collapsing everything into one vector store.

DESIGN OUTCOME

Continuity becomes a governed system, not a larger prompt.

The result is a companion that can carry a scene and a relationship across sessions while keeping identity, transient state, memory authority and conversational initiative independently inspectable. The research treats forgetting as a design decision: sometimes the correct behaviour is to recall, sometimes to wait for a cue, and sometimes to let an old topic stay closed.

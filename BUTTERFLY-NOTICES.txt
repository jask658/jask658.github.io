Butterfly motion technique references
====================================
The butterfly uses original procedural point surfaces and a local 3D body-axis hinge.
Reference techniques: spanwise phase-lagged wing rotation/projection (Yoichi Kobayashi),
and root-weighted wing deformation (Klevron). No external textures or models are used.

https://github.com/klevron/threejs-toys/blob/main/packages/toys/src/backgrounds/butterflies/index.js

PROJECT 04 / MOBILE ROBOTICS

All-terrain Mobile Robot

An Arduino-based mobile robot that combines multiple sensing channels with chassis control in a compact competition platform.

National second prizeDownload competition video ↗
SYSTEM INTEGRATION

Bring sensing, decisions and wheel actuation into one embedded loop.

In the 2025 China Robot and Artificial Intelligence Competition, our team received national second prize and Shanghai first prize in the All-Terrain Collaborative Robots event. I built hardware, integrated sensors, tuned operating parameters and modified state-machine logic within an existing framework during later-stage testing.

MULTI-SENSOR FUSION

Use complementary measurements instead of relying on a single signal.

The platform integrated grayscale, near-infrared, colour and tactile sensors. These complementary inputs supported the existing state-machine control framework; integration work focused on connecting the sensors, tuning operating parameters and checking behaviour on the competition course.

ARDUINO CONTROL

Translate fused state into repeatable mobile behaviour.

  1. Read and condition multiple sensor channels within the embedded control cycle.
  2. Combine the observations into a shared state used by the movement logic.
  3. Generate coordinated wheel commands for the compact four-wheel platform.
  4. Use onboard visual feedback during integration and competition debugging.
MECHANICAL PLATFORM

Traction and packaging shaped the final system.

Deep, segmented tyre blocks provide a pronounced contact profile, while the stacked chassis keeps the controller, wiring and display within a short wheelbase. Independent wheel-to-chassis connections keep the wheel geometry visually and mechanically distinct from the body.
